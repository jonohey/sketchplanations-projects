        How to play

        • Drag across touching letters to spell a word — up, down, sideways or diagonally. Let go to play it.
        • Each letter tile can be used once per word, and you can't skip over tiles.
        • Words need 3 or more letters. Qu sits on one tile and counts as two letters.
        • Slide back over the last letter to undo it.
        • Names and slang are kept in blue but don't score.
        • Tap a found word to see what it means.

        Options

        Pick a grid from 3×3 to 6×6, and play untimed or against a 1, 2 or 3 minute timer. Reroll tosses a fresh set of letters. Tap the timer to pause (the board hides until you resume, or choose Show board) and tap it again to carry on. The timer also pauses while this box is open. The share button copies a link to the current board, timer and your score, so friends can play the same letters and try to beat you. Settings has six colour schemes, all high contrast, including a dark one.

        Keyboard

        Type a word and press Enter. Or Tab to the grid, move with arrow keys, pick letters with Space and press it again on the last letter to play. Backspace undoes a letter, Esc clears.
